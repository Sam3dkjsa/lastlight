01 / THE PROBLEM

Good food can run out of time.

Small cafés make daily decisions about unsold, still-usable food. Without a quick shared record, the late shift may not know what is available, what needs attention first, or which safe next step is realistic.

THE GOAL

Make “use, share, or compost” easier to act on—and easier to account for.

02 / WHO & HOW

Made for the people closing up.

Intended users: café owners, shift leads, and kitchen or counter staff in small independent cafés.

Log a quantity and “use by” window, put the nearest deadline first, choose a workable route, then close the workflow when your team has acted.

04 / BOUNDARIES

Useful prototype. Honest limits.

  • Sample café items are synthetic examples, not records from a real business.
  • Non-weight units use rough demo conversions: 1 portion = 0.25 kg; 1 item = 0.18 kg.
  • Route success shares are assumptions: repurpose 90%, staff use 85%, discount 70%, donation 80%; compost/disposal 0%.
  • The default emissions factor is a user-editable placeholder, not a cited lifecycle assessment.
  • “Resolved” means the user closed a workflow—not that food was eaten, transferred, or independently verified.
  • Estimates omit transport, refrigeration, packaging, local grid mix, food type, and rebound effects. Actual impact may differ materially.
⌘

Tools used
Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; local browser storage; transparent arithmetic; built with the Manus web development environment. No external dataset or AI-generated impact claim is used.

Privacy & prototype constraints
Entries and the editable factor stay in this browser’s local storage. There is no account, shared team sync, database, or connection to a donation partner.
